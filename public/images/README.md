# Phase 1 hero illustration

`community-signals.png` is a decorative illustration generated using the built-in
imagegen tool. It is not a live signal map. The source desktop/mobile mockups were
inspected for visual direction and remain unchanged in `docs/design-references`.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a polished decorative web hero illustration for AfyaPulse, a Nigerian community-health app. Landscape composition, 1536x1024. Deep midnight navy background #08214A fading seamlessly to dark navy at all edges. Center a recognizable accurate outline silhouette of Nigeria as a subtle translucent blue holographic map, illuminated with thin cyan/teal lines, a few connecting network lines and small glowing nodes; symbolic connections only, no real data, no labels. Bottom quarter: lush green rolling landscape with sculptural Nigerian granite rock formations inspired by Zuma Rock, softly lit at dusk, elegantly illustrated in semi-realistic digital painting style. Keep the map clear and luminous, restrained futuristic glow, premium healthcare design, quiet atmosphere, high craft. The top third has generous navy negative space. No people, no buildings, no text, no numbers, no logos, no UI, no charts. This will sit alongside white text on a navy website hero and below the text on mobile.
